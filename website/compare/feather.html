What Quill adds

Updates itself

New Quill versions download, sign and install from Settings → Updates, with optional betas.

Checks before you sign

A pre-sign check warns about apps that need a newer iOS, an expired or revoked certificate, a free Apple ID near its weekly App ID limit, or too little storage. An entitlements viewer and a tweak checker catch more problems early.

Certificate alerts

Warnings before a certificate expires, background revocation checks with a notification, and a shortcut to the apps you need to re-sign.

Built into iOS

Home Screen widgets, Live Activities and the Dynamic Island, Siri and Shortcuts, and Spotlight search.

Backup and Transfer

Back up apps, certificates, sources and settings and restore them on another device.

Limits to know about

Quill is unofficial

It is a fork, not made or supported by Feather's developer. Report Quill problems to the Quill repository.

You still need a signing method

Quill needs a developer certificate (.p12 and .mobileprovision) or a free Apple ID, which gives 7-day apps. It runs on iOS and iPadOS 16 or later.

Common questions

Is Quill the same as Feather?

No. Quill is an unofficial fork of Feather by Samara, maintained separately by NuzFlameV2 (NuzProjects). It keeps Feather's core, on-device signing with your own certificate, and adds updates, safety checks and iOS integration on top. It is not made or supported by Feather's developer.

Why would I choose Quill over Feather?

Choose Quill if you want the app to update itself, warn you before a certificate expires or is revoked, check an IPA before you sign it, or back up and move your setup to another device.

Why would I stay on Feather?

If you want the original project from its own developer and do not need the extra features, Feather is a fine choice. Quill is a fork that adds features on top.

Where do I report a Quill problem?

At github.com/NuzProjects/Quill/issues, not to the original Feather project.
