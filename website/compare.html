Quill is a fork, so everything you know from Feather is still here.

Updates itself

New Quill versions download, sign and install from inside the app, with optional betas.

Checks before you sign

A pre-sign check, an entitlements viewer and a tweak checker catch problems before they cost you a certificate or a day.

Certificate alerts

Warnings before a certificate expires, background revocation checks, and a shortcut to the apps you need to re-sign.

Built into iOS

Home Screen widgets, Live Activities, Siri and Shortcuts, and Spotlight search for your library.

Backup and Transfer

Move your apps, certificates, sources and settings to another device.

Sources and library

GitHub release sources, library sorting and a customizable tab bar.

Which one should you pick?

Pick Quill if

You want to sign and install on the device with no computer, and you have a developer certificate (or want to try a free Apple ID).

Pick AltStore or SideStore if

You only have a free Apple ID, do not mind refreshing every 7 days, and want a store-style app with its own sources.

Pick Sideloadly if

You are happy to plug your device into a Mac or PC every time you install.
